1. Current values are already close for the Normal player. Normal escapes in 100% of 200 runs, median day 20 (IQR 19-21), about 115 real minutes, 1.7 collapses per run, and never a game over. It reaches zone 2 on day 4 (you reached it on day 3 knowing the map, so that matches). The problems are at the two ends: Beginners and pacing.
  2. Beginners die on days 13-15 because the pump stops on day 12. At State of Calamity the water table (7.5 m) goes below the pump's 7 m suction limit. Only 31% of Beginner runs had bought the Deep-Set Cylinder (W4) by then. 113 of 137 Beginner game overs are a collapse in the hub with no water coming in. The recommended setup softens this (water table 6.5 m, Stage 4 from day 18, W4 cheaper): Beginner game over 68% → 0%, escapes 18% → 54%.
  3. Skilled is too fast. The evolved bot escapes on day 10-11 (fastest day 6-7). It skips W4 and the Biosand Filter, pours from its own HYD down to the floor, and is out before the pump trouble on day 12. Even with the recommended values the fastest run is day 7 (median 14). No tested knob holds the fastest Skilled run at day 10 or later without pushing Normal past day 24. The real lever is structural: a minimum on when the exit can open, or a bigger first zone 2 exit lock that only the Jerry Can can carry in fewer trips.
  4. One build dominates. On current values "speed + hands first" (Light Sandals, Quick Hands) escapes around day 15, while every other order lands on 19-21. With the recommended prices "carry first" leads instead (day 14). Raising the Hydration Pack S4 to 40 L brings all 7 tested orders to days 19-21. Don't go to 45: that's 57 L after the ×1.25 rise, more than the 50 L Drum holds, and the bot skips it.
  5. Long mid-game drought of rewards. The median run's longest gap between "new things" is about 28-34 real minutes (target 12). It almost always ends with the zone 2 exit opening (149/200 runs) and starts around day 12. That stretch is spent ferrying water to the 16-18 L exit basin with nothing new to find. Ideas: show basin progress as a milestone, put a note or cache near each zone 2 exit, or make zone 2 rooms more worth visiting. Today rooms give only ~5 L per run, versus ~700 L from the pump.
  6. Dead time is 63%. Pumping 19% plus re-walking known corridors 44%. Every trip starts at the hub, so the walk out to zone 2 is repeated each day. No balance knob moves this much. It comes from the hub-and-spoke loop. Caveat: Normal bots never use the 8 valve grates (they only try one when nothing is left to explore), so shortcuts aren't used and this number is pessimistic.
  7. Water is not the bottleneck mid-game. From about day 8, Normal's Clean Storage sits near its cap and it buys every item on its list by day 23. Maze water rewards (tiles, sections, milestones, ~40 L per run) are small next to the pump (~700 L). The pump and the 0.167 L/s boiling pot set the pace early. Time and walking set it later.
  8. Minigames cost a lot of time. Normal wins about 25% of zone 1 exit valves (23° band, 157°/s plus speed-ups) and 20% of room doors. It averages 38 door attempts per run (free, but each takes about 5 s).
  9. Barely-used items and knobs. SODIS (P2) and Chlorine (P3) do nothing in the game code yet. Collapse penalties, lockdown drain, room water and crouch drain barely move the results (see the sensitivity ranking). Collapses are rare and almost never at night.
  10. Side effects of the recommended values. Beginners stop getting game overs (68% → 0%), but a run that doesn't escape collapses about 23 times and keeps going to day 40 (46% of runs). That's a "collapse treadmill": the working pump means storage never drops under 2 L, so a collapse costs little. If that feels wrong, raise the collapse costs (more unbanked loss or clock penalty; they barely change Normal) or add a cap on collapses. The Skilled bot was evolved on the current values. With the new ones its risky style (pouring HYD down to the floor) gives 14% game overs, so a re-evolved Skilled would be a bit slower and safer.
  11. Sim limit: valve grates. No bot ever opens a valve grate (0 tries). They only try one when nothing else is left to explore. So the 2 L rewards and the shortcuts the grates open are missing from every number here.

Bugs found in the sim while building it (all fixed, listed so you can trust the numbers): zone 2 exit basins were tied to the turning zone (in the game they sit on the fixed wall); bots kept pumping a Raw Basin the pot was draining; a hub-gate basin the bot gave up on never reset; and a bot could save forever for an item it could never afford with its reserve. Before the first fix, Normal looked like it escaped only 52% of the time. Don't use any numbers from before these fixes.
