## Team picks (2026-11-21)

The files above are the picks; the other options were deleted. How to use some of them:
- **Footsteps on sand:** mix `sfx_player_footstep_sand_01` and `_02` (alternate or pick at random per step).
- **Valve wheel:** play `sfx_gates_valve_03`, then `sfx_gates_valve_02` at the end of the turn.
- **Minigame fail:** `sfx_gates_fail_03`.
