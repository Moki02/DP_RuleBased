### How to use some of the picks
- **Gate opening:** `sfx_gates_doorrise_01`. **Room door opening:** `sfx_gates_gateslide_01`
  (swapped on purpose: the names are from what the files were first found for).
- **Valve wheel:** `sfx_gates_valve_03`. **Minigame fail:** `sfx_gates_fail_03`.
- **Footsteps on sand:** `sfx_player_footstep_sand_02` (one long recording; play as a loop
  while walking, or cut into single steps later).
- **Boiling:** `sfx_hub_boil_04` (loop). **Lockdown:** `sfx_world_lockdown_06` (warfare horn).
- **Music (BGM):** not ours; another team member handles it. All BGM files were removed.
- **Gate slam at night:** `sfx_gates_gateslam_04` (already layered for weight).
- **Footsteps on stone:** `sfx_player_footstep_stone_07` (one step; vary pitch slightly per step).
- **Low-HYD breathing:** `sfx_player_lowhyd_breath_02`. **Not enough water:** `sfx_ui_notenough_05`
- **Zone 2 turning:** `sfx_maze_zone2turnmix_01` (7.5 s, already layered; play once per turn).
- **Dusk warning:** `sfx_world_duskwarning_08` (one 2 s siren). **Dawn:** `sfx_world_dawn_06` (loop).
- **Tap running:** `sfx_hub_tap_06` (valve twist creak; play when the tap opens, then `sfx_hub_pour_02` as the running loop).
- **Collapse:** `sfx_player_collapse_06`. **Win:** `sfx_stinger_win_12`. **Game over:** `sfx_stinger_gameover_10`.
- **Cistern filling:** `sfx_maze_cistern_05` (splash + bubbling in one clip, 4.5 s).
- **Sprint breath:** `sfx_player_sprintbreath_03` (20 s; loop or play a section while sprinting). **Canteen fill:** `sfx_player_canteenfill_06`.
- **Pump:** `sfx_hub_pumploop_01` (loop while the player pumps; stop at the end of a stroke).
